# St. Thomas Mar Thoma Yuvajana Sakhyam, Pattor — Community Website

A public website (no logins) for the youth fellowship: what's coming up, who leads it, and how to get in touch.

## Pages

1. **Home** — welcome banner with the fellowship name and Pattor location, a line about Sunday worship and the meeting that follows, the next 2–3 upcoming events, a short "who we are" note, and a clear "Get in touch" button.
2. **Events** — upcoming gatherings and meetings as cards (date, time, place, short description), with past events listed below. Includes the weekly rhythm: Sunday service and the meeting after it.
3. **Execom** — the selected executive committee, each with photo placeholder, name and role (President, Vice President, Secretary, Joint Secretary, Treasurer, and committee members).
4. **Contact / Join us** — a simple form (name, phone or email, message) plus church address, service timings, and social links.

A shared header with navigation and a footer with contact details appear on every page.

## Content

I'll fill the pages with clearly-marked placeholder details — sample event dates, example execom names and roles, a placeholder address and phone number. Please send me the real names, roles, timings and contact details and I'll swap them in.

## Design

Before building I'll show you three visual directions to choose from — warm and traditional, or clean and modern — all with a reverent, youthful feel. Photos will be tasteful stock-style images until you share real ones.

## Notes

- Everything is public; no accounts or admin area for now. Events and execom details are kept in simple content files that are easy to edit later.
- The contact form will show a confirmation message; actually delivering messages to an inbox needs a backend, which we can add in a later step if you want.
- Works on phones and desktop, with page titles and descriptions set for search and sharing.
